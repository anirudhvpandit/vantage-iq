# KPI dictionary

All metrics are synthetic demonstration values. “Unavailable” means the denominator or evidence required for an honest calculation is absent.

| KPI | Formula | Source / period | Direction | Important limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recognized revenue | Sum of recognized transaction amounts (refunds may be negative) | Revenue transactions; selected dates | Higher | Not invoiced, booked, collected, or pipeline value |
| Operating costs | Sum of recorded expense amounts | Expense transactions; selected dates | Lower | Company-wide expenses are not allocated to departments |
| Operating profit | Recognized revenue − recorded operating costs | Revenue and expenses; selected dates | Higher | Not net profit or EBITDA |
| Operating margin | Operating profit ÷ recognized revenue × 100 | Same period | Higher | Unavailable when revenue is zero or negative |
| Revenue target achievement | Recognized revenue ÷ prorated monthly revenue target × 100 | Revenue + department monthly budgets | Higher | Monthly targets prorated by included calendar days; not a forecast |
| On-time delivery | Eligible completed projects delivered by planned end ÷ eligible completed projects × 100 | Project records | Higher | Incomplete projects excluded; no eligible projects means unavailable |
| Customer activity | Distinct customers with recognized activity ÷ total demo customers × 100 | Customer and revenue records | Higher | Activity proxy, not cohort retention, churn, recurring revenue, or CLV |

Comparison percentage change is `(current - previous) / abs(previous) × 100`. If the previous-period value is zero or predates the available synthetic history, the change is unavailable instead of infinite or misleading. Operating-margin comparison is a percentage-point difference, not a relative percent change.

Targets, named KPI owners, and arbitrary department expense allocations are not fabricated. Forecasts and net profit/EBITDA are not supplied.
