# Commercial roadmap

## Illustrative future plans

These are planning examples only. No billing system or entitlements exist.

| Plan | Illustrative limits | Possible capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 1 organization, 5 users, 100k records, 20 report exports/month | Core dashboards, CSV import, standard roles |
| Professional | 1 organization, 25 users, 1m records, 250 exports/month | XLSX/PDF reports, custom KPIs, scheduled alerts |
| Business | Multiple workspaces by contract, 100 users/workspace, negotiated data volume | Advanced access policy, audit exports, branding, priority support |

Limits must be represented by centralized server-side entitlement configuration rather than scattered plan-name checks. Any contract and plan matrix requires product, legal, finance, and security review before being marketed.

## Suggested sequencing

1. Identity, invite/reset lifecycle, server-side role checks, and tenant-isolation tests.
2. Alembic schema migration, audit events, persistent import history and reversal, and production operations.
3. Complete data workflows for project/customer/budget/KPI management.
4. Report generation (PDF/XLSX), export safety, access control, and browser-level quality.
5. Reconciled targets, supported accounting periods, currency policy, documented forecast methodology, and alert rules.
6. Privacy review, customer contracts, incident response, backup/restore drills, monitoring, and external security testing.
7. Only then assess payment integration and plan entitlements.

Do not claim GDPR, SOC 2, ISO 27001, or other compliance from this MVP.
